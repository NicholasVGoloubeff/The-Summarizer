What LectureNav does

Upload a PDF once. LectureNav builds a local index so you can:

Ask questions
“Explain this step”, “What does this theorem mean?”, “Walk me through this example.”
Get summaries
Turn dense slides into short, readable explanations in student-friendly language.
Generate practice
Create practice problems + answers based on the actual content of your lecture.
Keep a session history
Click previous Q&A, summaries, or practice sets to review them instantly.

How to use it

Just three steps:

  1. Go to the App tab and upload a PDF lecture.
  2. Select the lecture from the dropdown.
  3. Choose a mode: Q&A, Summary, or Practice and start studying.
